Venture Emergence

Venture Emergence simulates how entrepreneurs navigate uncertainty through framing, confidence updating, and social interaction. Each entrepreneur holds a limited frame — a subset of dimensions they attend to — and receives feedback based on how well their frame aligns with hidden stakeholder concerns.

The simulation builds up through 7 layers: L0 raw engagement, L1 frame-filtered perception, L2 confidence dynamics with effort drain, L3 anomaly-driven reframing, L4 experience effects, L5 social network influence, and L6 world-disclosing feedback where successful emergence reshapes what stakeholders value. Toggle layers on/off to isolate their effects.

Layers

L0: Engage, raw feedback

L1: Frame filters perception

L2: Effort drain + feedback → confidence

L3: Anomaly → reframe

L4: Experience modifies rates

L5: Social influence on confidence/reframe

L6: Success shifts stakeholder concerns
